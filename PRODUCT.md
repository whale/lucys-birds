# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lucy and her family use this as a personal field guide and visual record of the birds she has found. Visitors should be able to browse without instruction, while Lucy can add sightings and recordings through a simple protected flow.

## Product Purpose

Lucy’s Birds preserves a growing bird collection through illustrations, names, locations, and sound. Success means the collection feels inviting to explore, is quick to understand, and keeps Lucy’s own observations distinct from reference material.

## Brand Personality

Observant, editorial, warm. The experience should feel like a carefully kept naturalist’s notebook rather than a generic wildlife database.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard styling, dark filled cards, heavy rounded boxes, loud color, playful bounce, and decorative motion that delays browsing. Do not make the collection feel commercial or gamified.

## Design Principles

1. Let the bird illustrations carry the visual character.
2. Prefer fine rules and open space over filled containers.
3. Keep utility typography quiet and precise.
4. Make every interaction understandable without instructions.
5. Use motion to clarify spatial relationships, never as spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, touch-friendly controls, and a reduced-motion alternative. Responsive layouts must remain usable on phones and laptops without clipped content or unnecessary scrolling.
